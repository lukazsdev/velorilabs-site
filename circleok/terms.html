CircleOK terms of use

Last updated 3 November 2026

1. What CircleOK is, and is not

CircleOK helps a family notice when an older relative has not checked in, and keep shared notes about their care. It is not an emergency service, a medical device or a monitoring service. Notifications can be delayed or missed because of phone settings, networks, battery optimisation or outages. Do not rely on CircleOK as the only way of knowing a person is safe. In an emergency call 911 (US) or 999 / 112 (UK).

2. No medical advice

The medication logbook, appointments, documents and notes record what your family enters. CircleOK does not calculate doses, check interactions or give medical recommendations. Always follow the instructions of the prescriber and pharmacist.

3. Accounts and circles

Family members need an account; the parent's phone does not. You must have the parent's agreement to set the app up on their phone and to share their information with the circle. The circle owner decides who joins and may remove members or disconnect the parent's phone. You are responsible for what you upload; keep it to what your family needs.

4. Family plan subscription

Core check-ins and alerts to one family member are free. The Family plan (more members, SMS alerts, care tools) is a subscription billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play at the price shown in the app (currently $9.99 / £9.99 a month or $79.99 / £79.99 a year after a 7-day free trial). It renews automatically until cancelled in your App Store or Play account settings at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. If the subscription lapses, care-tool data stays read-only in your circle until you delete it.

5. Acceptable use

Do not use CircleOK to monitor someone without their knowledge, to harass, or to store content you have no right to share. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.
