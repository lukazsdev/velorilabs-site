Delete your CircleOK account

Developer: Velori Labs Ltd · App: CircleOK

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open CircleOK and sign in as a family member.
  2. Tap the profile icon, then Delete my account.
  3. Read what will be removed, switch on I understand, and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

By request (if you cannot open the app)

Email hello@velorilabs.co.uk with the subject "Delete my CircleOK account" from the email address you used to sign in (or the one linked to your Apple or Google sign-in). We confirm and delete within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, mobile number, sign-in and push notification tokens.
  • Your membership of every circle. Notes and tasks you added stay for the circle, without your name.
  • If you own a circle: the whole circle for everyone in it — the parent's connection, check-in history, alerts, medications and dose log, appointments, documents, notes and tasks. To keep the circle, hand it to another member first (Manage circle → Make owner).

Parent's phone

The parent's phone has no account. Its connection ends when the circle owner taps Disconnect in Manage circle, or when the circle is deleted. Removing the app from the parent's phone also stops it sending check-ins.

What is kept

Encrypted database backups are rotated within 30 days. Purchase records stay with Apple or Google under their policies. Nothing else is retained.
