Setting up

How do I connect my parent's phone?

In your app go to Manage circle → Connect parent's phone to get a 6-digit code. On the parent's phone open CircleOK, choose This is my phone and type the code. No password or email is needed on the parent's phone. The code expires after 15 minutes; generate a new one if needed.

Which phones work?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, Android 8 or later. The parent's phone needs notifications allowed so reminders and the lock-screen "I'm OK" button appear.

How do I add the home-screen or lock-screen button?

iPhone: touch and hold the home screen → Edit → Add widget → CircleOK. Android: touch and hold the home screen → Widgets → CircleOK. Tapping the widget checks in without opening the app.

Check-ins and alerts

When is a check-in "missed"?

The circle sets a daily time (for example 10:00) and a grace period (for example 60 minutes) in the parent's time zone. If no check-in arrives by the end of the grace period, every family member gets a push notification. On the Family plan the escalation contacts also get an SMS 15 minutes later if nobody has acknowledged the alert.

My parent checked in but the app says "pending"

The phone was offline. The check-in is saved on the phone and sent as soon as it has a connection; the family sees it then. If it stays pending for more than an hour, open the app on the parent's phone while it has Wi-Fi.

What does "I need help" do?

It alerts the whole circle immediately (push, and SMS on the Family plan) and shows the Call buttons. It does not call emergency services. In an emergency call 911 (US) or 999 / 112 (UK).

Reminders arrive late on Android

Android can delay alarms to save battery. In the parent's app open Settings → Reminders at the exact time and allow it, and exclude CircleOK from battery optimisation in Android settings.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. To restore on a new phone, open Settings → Family plan → Restore purchases in CircleOK, signed in with the same account that bought it.

Account

How do I delete my account or my parent's data?

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. Or follow the steps at velorilabs.co.uk/circleok/delete-account. To stop a parent's phone without deleting the circle, use Manage circle → Disconnect.
