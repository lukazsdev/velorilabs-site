CircleOK privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

CircleOK is a family check-in app made by Velori Labs Ltd ("we"), registered in England and Wales, company number 17529890. This policy explains what we store, why, and how to get it removed. We do not sell data, we do not show ads, and the app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Two kinds of users

Parents use the app without an account: a family member connects the parent's phone with a one-time code. The phone keeps a random device token; we store only a hash of it. Family members sign in with Apple, Google or an email link and form a "circle" around a parent.

What we store

Data Why
Family member name, email address, optional mobile number Sign-in, showing who did what in the circle, SMS alerts and the parent's "Call" buttons (mobile number only if you add it)
Parent's first name, check-in time and time zone Running the daily check-in and reminders
Check-ins, missed check-in alerts, "I need help" alerts, who acknowledged them and notes you add The core service: telling the family the parent is OK, or that they are not
Medications, dose log, appointments, documents (photos, PDFs), notes and tasks Only if your circle uses the Family plan care tools. This is information your family types or uploads. It may include health information; it is shared only with members of your circle
Push notification tokens, device platform Delivering alerts and reminders
Subscription status Unlocking the Family plan (we receive status from Apple / Google via RevenueCat, never card details)
Server logs: request ids, timings, error codes Keeping the service working. Logs never contain names, phone numbers, health text or tokens

Where it is processed

Data is stored with Supabase (database, file storage, authentication) in London (AWS eu-west-2). Push notifications pass through Expo's push service and Apple / Google. If your circle has SMS alerts enabled, phone numbers and the alert text are sent through Twilio. Subscriptions are managed by RevenueCat with Apple App Store or Google Play as the merchant. Each of these companies processes data on our instructions under their standard terms.

Who can see what

Only members of a circle can see that circle's data; this is enforced in the database for every request. The parent's phone can see its own status and the Call buttons the family set up, nothing else. Velori Labs staff do not look at circle content except to investigate a problem you have reported.

Retention and deletion

Data stays while your circle exists. A circle owner can disconnect a parent's phone at any time. Any family member can delete their account in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or by request at velorilabs.co.uk/circleok/delete-account. Deleting the owner's account deletes the circle, its files and its alert history for everyone in it. Backups are rotated within 30 days.

Not an emergency service

CircleOK sends notifications to family members. It does not contact emergency services and delivery can be delayed by phones, networks or settings. In an emergency call 911 (US) or 999 / 112 (UK).

Children

CircleOK is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and applicable US state laws you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted. Email hello@velorilabs.co.uk from the address you signed in with. UK users can also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

Changes

We will post updates here and, for material changes, in the app.
